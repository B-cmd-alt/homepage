Homemade yogurt.自制酸奶.

Heat the milk, cool it, then keep it warm until it sets.

先把牛奶加热，放凉，再保温到凝固。

82–85°C82–85°C180–185°F, hold 5–10 min180–185°F，保持 5–10 分钟
42.2°C42.2°C108°F, keep warm108°F 保温
5–8 h5–8 小时until it sets直到凝固

You'll need材料

  • Milk牛奶
  • Probiotic powder益生菌粉
  • Optional: condensed milk, sugar, or a tea bag可选：炼乳、糖或茶包

Steps步骤

  1. 01

    Heat加热

    Heat the milk to 82–85°C (180–185°F) and hold it there for 5–10 minutes.

    把牛奶加热到 82–85°C（180–185°F），保持 5–10 分钟。

  2. 02

    Flavor调味

    While it is hot, add condensed milk, sugar, or a tea bag, as you like.

    趁热按口味加入炼乳、糖或茶包。

  3. 03

    Cool降温

    Take it off the heat and let the milk come down to 42–43°C (108–109°F).

    离火，让牛奶降到 42–43°C（108–109°F）。

  4. 04

    Add the starter加菌粉

    Add the probiotic powder and mix it in well.

    加入益生菌粉，搅拌均匀。

  5. 05

    Keep warm保温

    Hold it at 42.2°C (108°F) for 5–8 hours.

    在 42.2°C（108°F）保温 5–8 小时。

  6. 06

    Check检查

    Tilt the jar. When the milk no longer flows out, the yogurt is done.

    把瓶子倾斜，牛奶不再流出来，酸奶就做好了。
