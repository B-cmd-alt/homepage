Beef and vegetables · Wok牛肉蔬菜 · 炒锅

Mala dry pot. (heat to taste)麻辣干锅（辣度可调）

Numbing and spicy, as hot as you like: use fewer dried chilies for a milder pot.

又麻又辣，辣度自己定：怕辣就少放干辣椒。

4 × 44 × 4dried chilies, four kinds四种干辣椒，各 4 个
3030 颗Sichuan peppercorns花椒
5 + 5 min5 + 5 分钟fry the base, then lid on炒香底料，最后加盖焖

You'll need材料

Aromatics

香料

  • Dried chilies, 4 kinds × 4 each干辣椒 4 种，各 4 个
  • Sichuan peppercorns × 30花椒 30 颗
  • Ginger, 6 slices姜片 6 片
  • Garlic, 2 cloves大蒜 2 瓣
  • Half an onion洋葱半个
  • 3 scallions, cut in sections葱 3 根，切段

Oil and sauce

油和酱

  • Herb butter, 15 g香草黄油 15 克
  • Olive oil橄榄油
  • Doubanjiang豆瓣酱
  • Soy sauce酱油

Into the pot

主料

  • Beef牛肉
  • Carrot胡萝卜
  • Celery芹菜
  • Mushrooms蘑菇
  • Cauliflower花菜
  • Quail eggs鹌鹑蛋
  • Rice cakes年糕

For the beef

腌牛肉

  • Baking soda小苏打
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉
  • Salt盐
  • Cooking wine酒
  • Soy sauce酱油

Steps步骤

  1. 01

    Prep the aromatics备香料

    Get out 4 kinds of dried chili, 4 of each, and 30 Sichuan peppercorns. Slice 6 pieces of ginger, peel 2 garlic cloves, cut half an onion, and cut 3 scallions into sections.

    准备 4 种干辣椒，每种 4 个，花椒 30 颗。姜切 6 片，大蒜 2 瓣，洋葱半个，葱 3 根切段。

  2. 02

    Fry the base炒香

    Medium heat. Put in 15 g herb butter and some olive oil, then everything from step 1. Fry for 5 minutes.

    中火。放入 15 克香草黄油和橄榄油，再把第 1 步的香料全部放进去，炒 5 分钟。

  3. 03

    Add the sauces加酱

    After the 5 minutes, add doubanjiang and soy sauce.

    5 分钟后，放豆瓣酱和酱油。

  4. 04

    Cut the vegetables切菜

    Cut the carrot, celery and mushrooms.

    切胡萝卜、芹菜、蘑菇。

  5. 05

    Marinate the beef腌牛肉

    Mix the beef with baking soda, cornstarch, salt, cooking wine and soy sauce.

    牛肉加小苏打、玉米淀粉、盐、酒、酱油拌匀。

  6. 06

    Blanch过水

    Blanch the cauliflower, quail eggs, rice cakes and beef in boiling water.

    花菜、鹌鹑蛋、年糕和牛肉过水。

  7. 07

    Stir-fry on high大火翻炒

    Turn the wok up to high heat. Add the carrot, celery and mushrooms, then everything you blanched.

    把油锅的火调大，加入胡萝卜、芹菜、蘑菇，再加入所有过水的东西。

  8. 08

    Cover加盖焖

    Put the lid on and let it cook for 5 minutes.

    盖上盖子焖 5 分钟。
