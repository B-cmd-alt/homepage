Low-temperature steak.低温牛排

One temperature. The thickness decides the time.

温度只有一个，时间看厚度。

55°C55°C131°F water bath131°F 水浴
2.5–4.5 h2.5–4.5 小时set by thickness按厚度定
2–5 cm2–5 厘米steak thickness牛排厚度

You'll need材料

  • Beef steak牛排
  • Salt盐
  • Black pepper黑胡椒
  • Rosemary, or any spice you like迷迭香，或任何你喜欢的香料
  • Thyme百里香
  • Peeled garlic去皮大蒜
  • Butter or oil黄油或油
  • Vacuum bag真空袋

Time by thickness厚度与时间

  1. 2 cm thick厚 2 厘米2.5 h2.5 小时
  2. 3 cm thick厚 3 厘米3 h3 小时
  3. 4 cm thick厚 4 厘米3.5 h3.5 小时
  4. 5 cm thick厚 5 厘米4.5 h4.5 小时

Steps步骤

  1. 01

    Season调味

    Season the steak with salt and black pepper. Add rosemary, thyme, or any spice you like.

    给牛排撒上盐和黑胡椒，再加迷迭香、百里香，或任何你喜欢的香料。

  2. 02

    Bag装袋

    Put the steak in a vacuum bag. Add peeled garlic and a piece of butter, or a little oil.

    把牛排放进真空袋，加入去皮大蒜和一块黄油，或少许油。

  3. 03

    Seal封口

    Vacuum the air out and seal the bag tight around the steak.

    抽出空气，把袋子紧贴牛排封好。

  4. 04

    Cook水浴

    Lower the bag into 55°C (131°F) water. Leave it for the time that matches the thickness.

    把袋子放入 55°C（131°F）的水中，按厚度对应的时间煮。
