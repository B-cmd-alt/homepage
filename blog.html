Evaluation · Memory System Evaluator

Three layered failure modes of LLM-as-judge for memory benchmarks

A soft LLM judge scores "I don't have enough information" as fully correct, inflating results by 6–22 points. The usual fix has blind spots of its own. And two LLM judges can agree with each other at κ = 0.85 while agreeing with a careful human at only κ = 0.50. Ends with a judge protocol that reaches κ = 0.87 against human labels.
