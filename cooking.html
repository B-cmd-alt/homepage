Recipe 01

Low-temperature steak

One temperature. The thickness decides the time.

55°C131°F water bath
2.5–4.5 hset by thickness
2–5 cmsteak thickness

You'll need

  • Beef steak
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Rosemary, or any spice you like
  • Thyme
  • Peeled garlic
  • Butter or oil
  • Vacuum bag

Time by thickness

  1. 2 cm thick2.5 h
  2. 3 cm thick3 h
  3. 4 cm thick3.5 h
  4. 5 cm thick4.5 h

Steps

  1. 01

    Season

    Season the steak with salt and black pepper. Add rosemary, thyme, or any spice you like.

  2. 02

    Bag

    Put the steak in a vacuum bag. Add peeled garlic and a piece of butter, or a little oil.

  3. 03

    Seal

    Vacuum the air out and seal the bag tight around the steak.

  4. 04

    Cook

    Lower the bag into 55°C (131°F) water. Leave it for the time that matches the thickness.

Recipe 02

Homemade yogurt

Heat the milk, cool it, then keep it warm until it sets.

82–85°Cheat, hold 5–10 min
42.2°C108°F, keep warm
5–8 huntil it sets

You'll need

  • Milk
  • Probiotic powder
  • Optional: condensed milk, sugar, or a tea bag

Steps

  1. 01

    Heat

    Heat the milk to 82–85°C and hold it there for 5–10 minutes.

  2. 02

    Flavor

    While it is hot, add condensed milk, sugar, or a tea bag, as you like.

  3. 03

    Cool

    Take it off the heat and let the milk come down to 42–43°C.

  4. 04

    Add the starter

    Add the probiotic powder and mix it in well.

  5. 05

    Keep warm

    Hold it at 42.2°C (108°F) for 5–8 hours.

  6. 06

    Check

    Tilt the jar. When the milk no longer flows out, the yogurt is done.
